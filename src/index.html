Details on hipinfer

C++, HIP/CUDA. GitHub, site, Colab2026 to present

  • Reached 92 to 95% of cuBLAS speed on Turing with a from-scratch prefill GEMM, in an LLM inference engine I built in HIP, one source for NVIDIA and AMD GPUs. Its WikiText-2 perplexity is within 0.003% of Hugging Face's.

3×the requests at a quarter of the cache

SubCache

KV-cache compression benchmark. Keeping a quarter of the cache fit 3× the requests and gave 2.8× throughput.

Play: keep 32 of 86 tokens and see if it remembers

Details on SubCache

Python, PyTorch. GitHub, site2026

  • Built an open-source KV-cache compression benchmark. On long coding-agent sessions, keeping 25% of the cache fit 3x more requests on one GPU and raised throughput 2.8x (64% lower cost per token), for a 5% rise in perplexity.
  • Benchmarked 9 eviction policies, including the published baselines H2O and R-KV, on real coding-agent sessions, then on LongBench and needle-in-a-haystack retrieval with Llama-3.1-8B and on MATH-500 with DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B.
  • Designed a submodular eviction method that beat H2O by 1.1 to 2.5% on repetitive agent sessions, then profiled it and found the bottleneck: at a 12.5% budget, choosing what to evict took 71% of decode time, against under 1% for H2O.
